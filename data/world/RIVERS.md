# River source

`earth_rivers.geojson` is the unmodified Natural Earth 1:110m rivers and lake
centerlines GeoJSON, downloaded from:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson

Natural Earth data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The map projects these lines into its existing equirectangular coordinates and
marks shared land-to-land hex edges crossed by a river. At this world scale,
these are approximate tactical crossings, not navigable waterways or detailed
bridge locations. Land remains passable on both banks.
