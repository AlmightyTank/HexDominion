# Anti-slop audit 001: Hex Frontlines v0.4 Slice F

Date: 2026-11-04
Scope: commits `141f840..d39b474` (Slice F feature commit `c7a82b7` plus docs `d39b474`)
Engine: Godot 4.7.2 stable, headless on Windows, 30-run averages where a number is given
Mode: AFTER (audit only, no production code was changed while writing this)

## How to read this list

Every finding has a number, the rule it breaks, a one-line reason, and the evidence.
Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
Balance and performance findings are marked with their own domain tags and name the brief
requirement they break, because the Slice F brief asked for both.

Nothing here has been fixed yet. Pick the numbers you want and only those numbers get touched.

## Findings

### 1. [Balance, HIGH] The rival AI builds no infantry at all
Rule broken: brief requirement "Produce mixed infantry and vehicle armies" and "Use infantry to capture territory" (C-5 evidence over claims).
Reason: with the Slice F hook the rival produced two tanks, one artillery, one helicopter and one fighter in 900 simulated seconds, and not a single rifle, gunner or rocket.
Evidence: `tests/audit_probe.gd` A/B run, `reachable_hook_off` produced `{rifle 2, gunner 1, tank 2}` while `reachable_hook_on` produced `{tank 2, artillery 1, helicopter 1, fighter 1}`.

### 2. [Balance, HIGH] The AI stops training ground units while it saves for a counter
Rule broken: brief requirement "Avoid building endless units without strategic purpose" and C-1 intentionality (the saving behaviour was written down but its cost was not).
Reason: `CombinedArmsAI.production()` returns true even when it buys nothing, and `RivalEconomyAI.plan()` returns on true, so every tick spent saving also skips the rifle and tank path.
Evidence: time to fill the 8 unit cap went from 15 s before the hook to 81 s with a reachable objective and 143 s with an overseas objective, measured over 900 ticks.

### 3. [Balance, MEDIUM] The counters consume the whole army cap
Rule broken: R-31 (every decision needs a one-line reason), the counter priority ladder has no budget note.
Reason: anti-air, artillery, helicopter and fighter take four of the eight cap slots, and the overseas branch adds transport, destroyer and patrol boat, so the ground army is starved of room.
Evidence: the overseas run produced `{landing_transport 1, destroyer 1, patrol_boat 1, helicopter 1, fighter 1}` and stopped at eight units with two slots left for the ground force.

### 4. [Balance, MEDIUM] The AI builds a transport it cannot fill
Rule broken: brief requirement "Build ships and transports when overseas targets are reachable" is only half met (C-4 resilience).
Reason: the landing transport is bought while the AI produces no infantry, so it sails empty and the amphibious plan cannot execute.
Evidence: overseas run, `landing_transport 1` with no infantry in the roster.

### 5. [Balance, MEDIUM] The fighter outclasses every ground answer
Rule broken: R-31 (no written reason for the balance).
Reason: the jet deals 70 damage per shot with a 3.0 armour counter, which is 210 DPS against armour for 240 credits, the best damage per credit in the roster, and only mobile anti-air and other jets can shoot it.
Evidence: `UnitCatalog.TYPES` fighter damage 70, cooldown 0.5, `UnitCatalog.counter` armour 1.5, `CombatState` air counter 3.0 for anti-air only.

### 6. [Balance, LOW] Mobile anti-air is a pure specialist
Rule broken: R-31 (the trade-off is not written down anywhere).
Reason: it deals 37.5 DPS against aircraft but 1.9 DPS against armour and 5 DPS against infantry for 140 credits, so it is dead weight against a ground-only enemy.
Evidence: `CombatState.advance` air counter 3.0, anti-air armour 0.15 and infantry 0.4.

### 7. [Balance, LOW] Artillery is the worst damage per credit against units and the best against buildings
Rule broken: R-31 (the trade-off is not written down).
Reason: 45 damage with a 0.5 armour counter is 7.5 DPS against armour for 200 credits, but the 3.0 structure counter gives 135 damage per 3 second shot, so it removes any building in a few shots.
Evidence: `UnitCatalog.TYPES` artillery damage 45, cooldown 3.0, structure 3.0.

### 8. [Balance, LOW] The AI never improves the army it already has
Rule broken: R-31.
Reason: each counter is guarded by `counts.get(kind, 0) == 0`, so once a helicopter exists the AI never buys a second one and never replaces a lost counter until it dies.
Evidence: `scripts/core/combined_arms_ai.gd` production guards.

### 9. [Performance, HIGH] The order system alone can exceed the frame budget
Rule broken: brief requirement "Large-army rendering performance" and "Keep AI calculations efficient enough for the hexagonal world map", plus R-35 (the feature must actually work at the stated scale).
Reason: `UnitOrders.update()` runs inside the physics frame and costs 2.9 ms at 50 units, 46 ms at 200 and 300 ms at 520 when those units hold attack-move orders, while the same roster without tactical orders costs 0.12 ms, 0.45 ms and 1.16 ms.
Evidence: 30 runs per size, Godot 4.7.2 headless, `tests/audit_probe.gd` PERF lines. The cost is the per unit threat scan over the whole roster and over every enemy building, run every physics frame.

### 10. [Performance, MEDIUM] The grouping cache check costs as much as the grouping
Rule broken: R-40 (performance mechanisms must be real).
Reason: `ArmyPresentation.refresh()` spends 1.0 ms per cached call and 4.3 ms per rebuild at 520 units, and it is called several times per draw through `military_batches()` and `unit_position()`.
Evidence: PERF `army_refresh_cached` and `army_refresh_rebuild` lines at n=520.

### 11. [Performance, MEDIUM] The order summary is rebuilt every frame
Rule broken: R-40.
Reason: `main.gd::_process` calls `show_orders()` on every frame, which rebuilds a Dictionary and a PackedStringArray and rewrites the label although the panel only changes when the selection changes.
Evidence: PERF `order_summary` line, 285 microseconds at 520 units per frame.

### 12. [Performance, LOW] The docs report a rebuild timing but not the per frame cost
Rule broken: R-36 (no performance claim without evidence).
Reason: `docs/V0.4-SLICE-F.md` and `README.md` quote the 520 unit grouping rebuild of 2095 to 5112 microseconds and say nothing about the order update cost, so the efficiency claim is not backed.
Evidence: the perf lines above.

### 13. [Accessibility, HIGH] The new unit count label fails contrast on every terrain colour
Rule broken: R-25 (WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1 for normal text).
Reason: the medium zoom count is drawn in `Color.WHITE` straight onto the terrain, which gives 1.20 to 1 on snow, 1.58 to 1 on sea ice, 2.35 to 1 on tundra, 2.58 to 1 on desert, 3.53 to 1 on mountain, 3.76 to 1 on city and 4.06 to 1 on plains.
Evidence: `scripts/units/unit_layer.gd` draws the count with `Color.WHITE`; ratios measured with the skill contrast script against the terrain palette in `scripts/world/world_map.gd`.

### 14. [Accessibility, HIGH] The order buttons sit below the fold in both layouts
Rule broken: R-03 (mobile layout must be perfect) and R-35 (verify before delivering).
Reason: the panel is appended after the selection text inside the bottom context scroll area, and the selection panel measures 0 px tall at 1152x648 and at 480x960 while the new panel needs 120 px, so the Attack-Move, Guard and Patrol buttons are only reachable by scrolling that region.
Evidence: `tests/audit_ui_probe.gd` measured `scroll_size=(321, 0)`, `panel_size=(313, 120)`, first button 126x44 and `first_button_visible=false` in both layouts.

### 15. [Accessibility, HIGH] The new controls have no pointer cursor
Rule broken: R-39 (every enabled control shows that it can be used).
Reason: `StrategyHUD._set_command_cursors()` runs inside the HUD's own `_ready()` and `main.gd` creates the panel afterwards, so the three order buttons and the rally checkbox keep the arrow cursor while every other HUD button shows a pointing hand.
Evidence: measured `mouse_default_cursor_shape = 0` on the first button and on the checkbox in both layouts.

### 16. [Accessibility, MEDIUM] The order buttons stay live with nothing selected
Rule broken: R-26 (a control that cannot do anything is a defect) and C-2.
Reason: the panel never disables itself, so pressing Attack-Move with an empty selection issues zero orders and reports "attack move: 0 accepted, 0 rejected", while the existing HUD disables its attack button when the roster is empty.
Evidence: `scripts/ui/military_orders_panel.gd` has no enabled state, against `attack_button.disabled = roster.is_empty()` in the HUD.

### 17. [Accessibility, MEDIUM] The rally toggle explains itself only in a tooltip
Rule broken: R-39 and R-32 (information cannot be mouse only).
Reason: the checkbox label says "Rally: attack-move" and the rest of the meaning sits in a tooltip that touch players never see, and the control sits in the unit selection inspector although it configures a production facility.
Evidence: `scripts/ui/military_orders_panel.gd` tooltip text only.

### 18. [Accessibility, MEDIUM] The panel border misses the non text contrast bar
Rule broken: R-39 and WCAG 1.4.11 (3 to 1 for component boundaries).
Reason: the CommandTheme border #526779 measures 2.49 to 1 on the panel and 2.05 to 1 on the button fill, so the boundaries of the new buttons are faint for low vision players.
Evidence: contrast script on #526779 against #1c2a38 and #25384a. The theme is pre-existing, the new surface is not.

### 19. [Accessibility, MEDIUM, pre-existing] The far zoom count repeats the same contrast defect
Rule broken: R-25.
Reason: the strategic tier draws its count in #f5efdb with no backing, which gives 1.05 to 1 on snow, 2.04 to 1 on tundra, 2.25 to 1 on desert and 3.53 to 1 on plains, so one fix should cover both tiers.
Evidence: `UnitAppearance.strategic` draws the count with `Color("f5efdb")`.

### 20. [Accessibility, LOW] The panel has no refused state
Rule broken: R-27 (empty, loading and error states).
Reason: the panel shows an empty state and an order summary, but a refused order is only reported in the transient command info line, so the panel itself never says that an order was rejected.
Evidence: `scripts/main.gd` `_issue_tactical` reports rejections through `hud.show_command_info`.

### 21. [Antislop code, LOW] A comment restates the block and the zoom thresholds have no reason
Rule broken: R-31 (every decision needs a reason, comments must add information).
Reason: the line "Army view: a few small sprites per role with the true unit count." repeats what the code shows, and the 0.65 and 1.5 tier thresholds have no written reason anywhere.
Evidence: `scripts/units/unit_layer.gd` far zoom block.

### 22. [Antislop code, LOW] The button label dictionary is written twice
Rule broken: R-31 (one source of truth) and C-1.
Reason: `military_orders_panel.gd` writes the same three label dictionary once to iterate the buttons and again to look up the text, so the two can drift.
Evidence: `scripts/ui/military_orders_panel.gd` `configure`.

### 23. [Antislop, LOW] The new interface has no recorded design direction
Rule broken: R-37 (design direction required) and R-31.
Reason: the repository has no DESIGN.md and no line records why the panel sits in the inspector, why the grid is two columns, or why the order summary uses that wording.
Evidence: repository root and `docs/` contain no direction file.

### 24. [Antislop copy, PASS] No em dash in the Slice F work
Rule checked: R-02.
Result: `git diff 141f840..d39b474` contains zero U+2014 characters, so the new copy, docs and code comments are clean.
Evidence: character count over the diff.

## Verified passes

- R-02: no em dash in the Slice F diff, see finding 24.
- R-03 tap targets: the three order buttons measure 126x44 and the rally checkbox 44 px tall, so the minimum size holds.
- R-32: the order keys Z, V and O work from `_unhandled_input`, Escape cancels a pending order, the buttons are native Godot controls that take focus and answer Enter and Space, and the focus accent #79b8e8 on the button fill measures 5.64 to 1.
- R-26: with a selection, every new control performs a real action, the order summary reflects the real per order counts, and the rally checkbox reaches `EconomyState.set_rally` with the attack-move flag.
- R-27: the panel has an empty state, "Select units to issue orders." Finding 20 covers the missing refused state.
- R-31 partly: the tricky order rules carry one line reasons, for example "Finish the current edge before stopping. Neither combat nor resuming teleports." and "Save toward the chosen counter instead of spending the same credits on rifles."
- R-33, R-34: no source or CSS is patched by an external script and no theme toggle was added, so there is no second theme to break.
- R-35: the project runs headless with no console errors, the full suite is green in CI, and the numbers in this document come from executed runs rather than inspection.
- R-36: the shipped docs quote only measured numbers and label the 520 unit figure as a rebuild timing rather than a frame rate.

## Measurement method

The performance and AI numbers come from two headless probe scripts, `tools/audit_probe.gd` and
`tools/audit_ui_probe.gd`. They are untracked tooling, not part of the committed suite, and the
method behind them is:

- performance: build N rifle units on an all land axial map, give each an attack-move order,
  run `UnitOrders.update` 30 times and report microseconds per call, then repeat with the
  tactical orders cleared, then measure `ArmyPresentation.refresh` with an unchanged roster
  and with a fresh instance, then measure the order summary loop.
- AI: a split map with land at q 0 to 5 and q 20 to 25, the rival owning the west land, a
  player headquarters on the east land for the overseas case and on the west land for the
  reachable case, the rival starting with 800 credits, a headquarters, barracks, power plant
  and factory, then 900 ticks of `RivalEconomyAI.plan` plus `EconomyState.advance(1.0)`.
- interface: instantiate `scenes/main.tscn` headless at 1152x648 and at 480x960, then read
  `selection_panel.size`, `military_orders_panel.size`, the first button rect and
  `mouse_default_cursor_shape`.

## Open questions for the owner

1. Finding 9 is the serious one: the fix is to stop scanning the whole roster for every
   ordered unit every physics frame, for example by scanning once per frame and sharing the
   result. That changes simulation behaviour and needs a decision.
2. Findings 1 to 4 point at one cause: the counter hook runs before the normal training
   path and returns true even when it buys nothing. A fix could reserve the counters out of
   the army cap instead, or let the normal training path run while the AI saves.
3. Findings 13 and 19 want a backing colour behind the count text, which changes the map
   look, so it is a design call rather than a bug fix.
