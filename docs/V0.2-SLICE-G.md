# v0.2 Slice G: Power system

Construct power plants from the sidebar or press P to select their placement ghost. Each costs 150 CR, takes 8 seconds to build, has 250 HP, and supplies 40 power when completed, controlled and uncontested. Plants use the existing placement, construction, cancellation and save rules. Cancellation refunds 75 CR.

## Supply and consumption

| Completed structure | Supply | Demand | Low-power effect |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Headquarters | 20 | 0 | HQ income unchanged |
| Power plant | 40 | 0 | Supplies power |
| Barracks | 0 | 0 | Rifle production unchanged |
| Factory | 0 | 20 | Tank production runs at 50% speed |
| Extractor | 0 | 10 | Extraction income disabled |

The header shows power used versus available supply and an explicit LOW/OK status. The inspected factory or extractor explains its low-power effect. Plants have a twin-tower silhouette and a health bar.

Power is faction-wide, with no cable placement or range requirement. Supply and demand are derived from current building and territory state rather than cached reservations. Unfinished, lost and contested structures contribute neither supply nor demand. Demand remains reserved by completed controlled factories and extractors even when their queues are empty or power is insufficient, preventing on/off oscillation. Equal supply and demand is sufficient; an empty network is not low power.

## Failure and recovery

Completed power plants can be attacked by stationary enemy units, with the existing unit-target priority. Destroying one removes supply immediately; combat resolves before economic production on the live simulation tick. Losing or contesting its hex also removes supply. Rebuilding, recapturing or clearing contest restores supply as soon as the plant is operational again.

Factories retain their paid queues and elapsed work during outages. Construction, barracks, HQ income and city income do not depend on power, so recovery does not require an already-functioning grid. Extractors are the currently implemented advanced structures disabled by low power; no new defense type is introduced in this slice. AI uses the same economy rules, but its existing rifle-focused planner does not construct plants automatically.

Simulation steps split at construction completion times, so recovery partway through a large tick affects only the remaining portion, regardless of building insertion order. Saves persist plants, health, construction state and queues; supply/demand are recomputed after loading. Older saves derive their power from their existing structures and may enter low power if demand exceeds HQ supply.

## Verification

Headless checks cover both factions, normal and low-power operation, completed-plant destruction through combat, immediate extraction shutdown, slowed factory queues, recovery through construction and recapture, contested supply, isolation between factions, and mid-tick recovery timing. Save/load regression coverage includes a damaged completed plant and restored derived power. Existing construction/cancellation tests also cover power plants.

Interactive checks remain pending: inspect the header and structure warnings, destroy a plant while a factory produces, verify extractor shutdown, restore supply, and check sidebar layout with the fifth construction choice.
