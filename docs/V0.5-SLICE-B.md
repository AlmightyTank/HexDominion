# v0.5 Slice B: Physical Supply Collection

## Scope

Slice B turns the approved economy boundary from Slice A into a playable physical delivery loop. Supplies are finite world stock, not a passive rate. A truck must reach a deposit, finish loading, survive the return trip and finish unloading at an operational friendly Supply Center before the faction receives credits.

Oil derricks, city development, research, radar and fog remain outside this slice.

## Balance contract

| Item | Implemented value |
| --- | --- |
| Small deposit | 1,500 supply |
| Medium deposit | 4,500 supply |
| Large deposit | 9,000 supply |
| Supply Truck | 200 CR, 6 seconds, 100 HP |
| Truck capacity | 150 supply |
| Loading | 3 seconds |
| Supply Center | 300 CR, 8 seconds, 300 HP, 10 power |
| Unloading | 2 seconds |
| Qualified road bonus | +10% for that delivery |

The Supply Truck is a logistics unit. It cannot attack, capture territory, carry infantry, board an APC or use a landing transport.

## Deposits and stable identity

Every existing world resource site becomes a deterministic small, medium or large finite deposit. Each deposit receives a stable axial ID in the form `supply_<q>_<r>`. New matches also place one equivalent nearby medium deposit for each faction so both starts have the same headquarters distance and initial stock opportunity.

Deposit state stores its size, capacity, remaining stock and original terrain. The world marker shows remaining stock and changes to a depleted mark at zero. Ownership alone does not generate credits.

## Authoritative truck lifecycle

The truck state is stored on `UnitState`, independent of its artwork:

1. `idle`: choose a reachable friendly uncontested deposit.
2. `to_deposit`: follow the existing terrain-aware A* route.
3. `loading`: remain at the deposit for 3 seconds.
4. Loading completion atomically withdraws up to 150 supply. A final partial load is valid. No credits are paid.
5. `to_center`: carry the cargo along a real route to an operational center.
6. `unloading`: remain at the center for 2 seconds.
7. Unloading completion submits the cargo once to `EconomyState.credit_approved_income()` and clears it only after approval.

`waiting_deposit` and `waiting_center` preserve a blocked assignment without inventing stock or credits. Automatic selection uses deterministic reachable candidates. A player can select only Supply Trucks and right-click or tap a deposit or friendly Supply Center to override the assignment.

If a center is destroyed, captured, contested, incomplete or disabled by a brownout, unloading does not pay and the truck keeps its cargo. It can retry or use another operational center. If the truck dies, its undelivered cargo disappears with it.

## Delivery payout and bonuses

The base payout equals the unloaded cargo. Pickup never calls the payout API.

At pickup, the simulation stores the actual deposit-to-center path. At unload time, `SupplyNetwork.qualifies_delivery_route()` checks that every sequential route hex is walkable, friendly-owned, uncontested and roaded. Only then does the delivery receive the +10% road bonus. Being near a road or connected to an unrelated road is not enough.

Continent bonuses remain additive:

```text
payout = cargo * (1 + road_bonus + continent_bonus)
```

The approved payout creates one account transaction and one income event. Repeated simulation ticks, inspection and save restoration cannot repeat it.

## Construction, power and repair

Supply Trucks use the existing factory production queue and wallet. Supply Centers use the existing placement, construction, cancellation, power, damage, repair and sale systems. A center is operational only when it is complete, alive, friendly-controlled, uncontested and the faction is not in a brownout.

## Interface and world feedback

- Deposit inspection reports size, remaining stock and depletion.
- Center inspection reports operational or offline state.
- Truck selection reports cargo, lifecycle state, timer and current assignment.
- World markers show finite stock and depletion.
- The supply panel explains that income requires a completed physical delivery and that the road bonus requires an all-road route.
- Manual assignment uses the existing desktop right-click and touch tap destination flow.

## Save compatibility

Match saves use schema version 3. The snapshot stores:

- every deposit ID, size, capacity, remaining stock and original terrain;
- truck cargo, task, timer and assignment;
- assigned center and the delivery route used as payout evidence; and
- the existing approved-income ledger and events.

Validation rejects unknown deposit IDs, duplicate IDs, invalid capacities, excess cargo, unknown phases, impossible timers, invalid routes and non-trucks carrying logistics state before mutating the live match.

Version 2 saves migrate in memory. Legacy 5,000-unit deposits become medium 4,500-capacity deposits, with remaining stock capped rather than increased. Existing trucks receive safe idle logistics defaults. Version 1 continues through the existing v1 to v2 migration before v3 is applied. Migration never injects missing starter assets into an old match.

## Verification

The release gate uses Godot 4.7.2:

```powershell
godot --headless --path . --import
godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/run_smoke_tests.gd
godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/combined_arms_tests.gd
godot --headless --path . --quit-after 5
```

Automated coverage includes the full round trip, no payout at pickup, exact-once unload, qualifying road payout, additive continent bonuses, contested deposits, simultaneous atomic pickup, final partial loads, depletion, truck destruction, offline and alternate centers, brownouts, manual assignment, production values, noncombat restrictions, v1/v2 migration and exact save restoration for all seven lifecycle states.

## Known limitations and next work

- Automated routing is deterministic and functional, but advanced economic AI, route defense and coordinated raids remain Slice D work.
- Fog-aware discovery does not exist until Slice F, so automation currently evaluates the authoritative world state.
- Balance values need full human playtesting over complete matches.
- Rendered desktop and physical touch-device acceptance remain separate from headless simulation verification.
- Slice C adds neutral capturable oil derricks as the first passive recurring income source.
