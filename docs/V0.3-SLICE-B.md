# v0.3 Slice B: Roads and Transportation

Choose **Road** in the construction sidebar's Build tab.
Click consecutive owned hexes to complete a highway. Each successful placement
costs 100 CR, completes immediately, and leaves the tool active. Right-click or
Escape cancels. Tap placement uses the existing touch gesture safeguards.

## Rules

- Owned, uncontested plains, forests, deserts and cities support roads. Resource
  locations use their underlying biome. Mountains, snow, tundra and ocean do not.
- Roads can share a hex with any structure, including headquarters and factories.
- Duplicate, invalid, unaffordable and off-map placements do not spend credits.
- Travel between two adjacent road hexes takes normal terrain-adjusted time
  divided by 1.3. Both infantry and tanks benefit, regardless of faction.
- Terrain and crossing penalties remain; roads are not bridges.
- Weighted pathfinding automatically considers road speed for new movement and
  rally orders. Existing routes keep their waypoints, but use live road speeds.
- Roads persist through capture, building destruction and sale. They have no
  upkeep, power demand, health, refund or removal action in this slice.
- Saves include the shared road network. Older saves load with no roads; invalid
  road data is rejected before changing the live match.
- Rival units use roads automatically. The AI does not yet budget for building them.

## Rendering and interface scope

Roads are continuous center-to-center strokes, beneath structures and units.
A dark edge separates the pale roadway from terrain; junctions join at hex
centers, with a small endpoint for isolated road cells rather than an icon.
The road tool reuses the current sidebar and placement colors. No external road
assets were introduced. Sidebar direction is recorded in RTS-HUD.md; the
requested afterward audit and interactive visual acceptance remain pending.

## Connectivity API for Slice C

- `RoadRules.has_road(tiles, hex)` checks eligible land and a completed road.
- `RoadRules.neighbors(tiles, hex)` returns adjacent road cells in stable hex
  direction order, shared by the visual connection renderer.
- `RoadRules.connected(tiles, start, goal)` checks the physical road network.
- Pass `territory, faction` as the final arguments to require an entirely
  friendly, uncontested network. Captured roads remain physically connected but
  no longer count toward the former owner's supply network.

Queries are derived from live tiles and ownership, without a stale connectivity
cache. No supply income, delivery vehicles or manual transportation is added.
Same-cell connectivity requires a road; missing cells and disconnected endpoints
return false. Movement retains public access to physical roads for either army.

The road option integrates into the pending RTS Build sidebar and its minimap
dependencies. Visual acceptance of that interface remains pending. No hosted CI
workflow exists in this repository; headless import, tests and boot are the local
validation gates.
