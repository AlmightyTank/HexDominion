All infantry use barracks, capture territory, receive forest infantry cover and can occupy the existing two-slot bunker. Tanks remain factory-produced. Damage multipliers apply to unit targets; structures receive base weapon damage, terrain attack modifiers remain unchanged. Weapons accumulate elapsed firing time only with an eligible target, fire after a full cooldown, retain fractional elapsed time across orders and never bank idle time. Damage resolves simultaneously. Movement suppresses firing; Hold permits automatic fire without pursuit.

AI production includes one anti-armor soldier when enemy armor exists and a machine gunner once it has two riflemen, subject to the existing money reserves, infrastructure priorities and eight-unit budget. Defensive AI accepts every infantry role.

## Orders and selection

Right-clicking enemy units/buildings issues an attack; X or the Attack button arms target selection for mouse/touch. Pursuit follows a living target and trims its route to weapon range. Explicit unit targets take priority over other unit targets; in-range unit fights still take priority over structures. Destroyed targets end pursuit with Hold. Move/Hold/garrison cancels pursuit, and accepted attacks release Hold/bunker assignment. Invalid/friendly/unreachable orders do not erase working orders. No attack-move or projectile simulation is added.

Shift-click or Add selection toggles individual members; left drag selects all friendly members beneath rendered groups. Repeated clicks on one grouped marker cycle its individual roster members. Close zoom uses individual rendered positions. Touch retains drag/pinch gestures and tap-to-move. The Inf tab shows composition and primary unit HP, damage, cooldown, range, speed and order state; production tooltips expose counters.

## Shared appearance architecture

- UnitCatalog registers appearance families. UnitAppearance draws forward-facing local geometry and common health/selection/order/feedback cues. Existing tanks use a separate tracked hull/turret silhouette through the same entry point.
- Soldier geometry includes split boots, tapered shoulders, backpack, faceted helmet and faction shoulder accents. Standard rifle, broad shoulder launcher with flared nose, and long machine gun/bipod/ammunition box have different geometry, not just different colors or letters.
- UnitVisuals builds presentation-only groups by last-reached hex, faction and role. It never creates, removes, merges or damages simulated units. Positions use deterministic roster slots; no collision footprint is implied.
- Far zoom below 0.65: different geometric role markers and real member counts, facing ticks, damage/attack feedback and aggregate minimum-health bars when selected/damaged.
- Far and medium zoom: one circular infantry marker per faction/type group, with an exact count when more than one unit is present. Mixed roles/factions occupy distinct slots.
- Close zoom: every infantry unit uses the same-size circular marker, faction-colored outer ring and upright rifle, rocket-launcher or heavy-machine-gun symbol. Selection adds a highlighted outline; damage adds a small health indicator. No humanoid geometry, breathing or walking animations are used. These shared drawing paths apply to desktop and mobile.

Visual decisions retain the user's existing RTS direction (`docs/RTS-HUD.md`, ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 for interface chrome). Dark circular infantry backgrounds separate pale weapon symbols from terrain; faction rings identify ownership and yellow outlines identify selection. Infantry icons remain upright for small-size readability. Vehicles retain tracked top-down silhouettes at every zoom. Markers use lightweight procedural drawing with no textures or paid assets. Session antislop mode remains afterward audit; no unrelated audit fixes are included.

## Saves

Version-1 saves remain readable. Optional weapon elapsed, facing, attack-order flag, destination and roster target index reconstruct the new state; missing fields default to neutral/zero. New roles and production queues use catalog validation. Live targets use weak references to avoid reference cycles, restored only after the complete replacement roster exists. Invalid role health, nonfinite timers and invalid target indexes reject the whole snapshot before mutation. Visual groups, phases and flashes are derived/transient, not authoritative saved state. Editing catalog tuning changes future loaded derived stats just like the earlier tank configuration; arbitrary runtime tuning overrides are not separately serialized.

## Outstanding limits and acceptance

- Physical touchscreen, full keyboard focus walkthrough and device-specific viewport/contrast acceptance remain pending. The existing narrow RTS sidebar scrolls; no unrelated mobile redesign is claimed.
- Screen-sized role groups may cover nearby hexes at far zoom; dense close-up formations can spread outside their simulation hex. A selected group's ring means at least one member, not all members, is selected. Counts always show the full group's real population.
- Close-up procedural art is deliberately lightweight. Grouped medium sprites summarize three representatives rather than pretending those are all simulated members. No new vehicle/air/naval mechanics are implemented.
- Cooldown balance and large army playtests remain needed. Combat uses last-reached hex positions, as before; no projectile travel, line-of-sight or collision avoidance.
- Full afterward design audit remains pending, and no comprehensive accessibility/design gate pass is claimed.
