# v0.2 Slice E: Construction progress

Paid placement creates a foundation, not an operational building. Costs are charged once on placement; destruction gives no refund. A foundation occupies its hex immediately and blocks further placement.

| Building | Build time | Health |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Headquarters | 12 seconds | 600 |
| Barracks | 6 seconds | 300 |
| Factory | 10 seconds | 450 |
| Extractor | 5 seconds | 200 |

Construction advances on simulation ticks while the original faction controls uncontested land. Loss or contest pauses progress; recapture resumes it. Pausing the game also pauses construction. Damage does not reset progress, and completion does not heal damage.

Unfinished HQs and extractors produce no building income. Independent city income still applies. Barracks and factories cannot accept training orders until complete. Oversized ticks award HQ/extractor income only for the portion after completion.

## Feedback and attacks

Foundations reveal the building silhouette as progress increases. A lower construction bar and upper health bar distinguish progress from damage. Inspecting an owned foundation shows elapsed/required time, health and paused status in the sidebar. Both factions use the same construction rules and visuals.

Stationary enemy units within attack range automatically target foundations of every type when they have no unit target. Moving units do not fire; unit combat retains priority. Zero health removes the foundation immediately, before construction advancement. Completed extractors remain destructible as before; completed non-extractor buildings retain the previous combat rules. HQ capture remains the match victory condition.

## Persistence and verification

Snapshots store construction elapsed time and remaining health in each building record. Loading validates timers, health and the absence of unfinished production queues before mutating the match. Saves from before this slice treat buildings without construction state as completed, preserving existing matches. Scenario starter buildings are also completed explicitly.

Headless tests cover all four timers, operational gating, pauses on contest/loss, damage and destruction during construction, no completion healing, and a partially built damaged extractor passing through the existing disk-save/load and deterministic continuation tests. Invalid timer ranges and unfinished production queues are rejected.

Interactive visual acceptance remains pending: inspect foundations at several zoom levels, watch completion, attack an unfinished structure, and save/relaunch/load midway through a build.
