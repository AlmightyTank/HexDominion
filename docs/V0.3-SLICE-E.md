# v0.3 Slice E: Fortifications, chokepoints and defense AI

This completes the implemented v0.3 Territorial Strategy milestone: terrain,
roads, supply connections, continent income and defensive positions. Interactive
acceptance and balance playtesting remain pending.

## Bunkers

- Cost 100 CR, build time 8 seconds, maximum health 400, no power demand.
- Use the existing construction tab, controlled uncontested land, empty footprint,
  foundations, cancellation, repair (1 CR/HP), sale (50 CR), destruction and saves.
- Mountains and narrow land bridges are legal. Bunkers are passable, including
  under tanks, and can share a road but not another building. No new path blocks,
  road interruption, autonomous guns, supply consumption or income are introduced.
- Stationary living friendly rifle squads can garrison a completed friendly bunker
  using G or the sidebar Garrison button. Capacity is two squads, not two soldiers.
  Armor, unfinished sites, enemy buildings and overflow assignments are rejected.
- Sheltered squads take 60% of otherwise applicable damage. This multiplies Slice A
  cover: a mountain rifle takes 0.75 * 0.60 = 45% of normal damage. Only the first
  two valid roster occupants receive protection, even for malformed excess flags.
- Moving releases the slot. Selling, destruction, ownership loss or loss of a valid
  slot clears the assignment on the next combat update. Holding remains active.
  Rebuilding does not automatically reassign soldiers. There is no death/ejection
  damage when a bunker is removed. Simultaneous combat uses tick-start protection.
  Captured structures retain their original infrastructure faction, as in earlier
  slices; capturing an enemy bunker does not transfer its management or shelter.

## Defensive orders and display

J or the sidebar Hold button orders selected units to stop at their last reached
hex. A partially traversed edge is cancelled; no free forward movement is awarded.
Held units fire within their normal range and never chase. A successful new move
order cancels hold and garrison; rejected moves preserve the current order. There
is no separate attack-order controller in this version; stationary auto-fire is
the existing real-time combat system. G toggles selected infantry assignments;
releasing a garrison leaves Hold active until a move order.

The existing faction-colored badge and health bar identify bunker ownership and
condition. Its low rectangular silhouette and firing slit distinguish it from
production buildings. The label shows stationed squads out of two. A bar beneath
a unit marks Hold; a square marks garrison. An exclamation above an isolated bunker
uses the existing HQ supply graph, without imposing new supply penalties. These
restrained marks retain the current RTS HUD direction and carry actual state.

## AI

The existing one-second decision timer runs a bounded defense planner. With at
least three living units and 350 CR available after economic planning, it can
buy one bunker that decision, retaining at least 250 CR. At most two original
faction bunkers, including foundations/lost structures, may exist.

At most two infantry and at most half the living army are reserved. Owned HQ comes
first; threatened extractors, cities, bunkers and roads receive higher priority.
Defenders travel to their site, hold when they arrive, and garrison where possible.
Other units retain expansion/combat behavior and can raid nearby structures with
at most one nearby defender. The last surviving unit remains free to expand.
This is a simple deterministic heuristic, not front lines or chokepoint analysis.
It considers owned sites and the small roster on decision ticks, not every frame.

## Saves and verification

`holding` is an optional validated boolean in unit snapshots, defaulting to false
for old saves. Existing garrison flags and normal building dictionaries persist;
shelter and capacity are reconstructed from live occupants, never cached in saves.
Roads, supply and continent bonuses retain their existing authoritative sources.

The full headless suite covers construction, capacity (including forged excess
flags), both factions, tank rejection, terrain stacking, retreat without pursuit,
movement cancellation, re-entry, repair/sale/destruction/capture cleanup, path and
road compatibility, continent preservation, AI spending/limits/raiding, HUD signals
and real match save/load. Existing economy, terrain, supply, continent, full-match,
backup/corruption and determinism tests also run.
