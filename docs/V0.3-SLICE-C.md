# v0.3 Slice C: Supply Connections and Income

## Rules

Every completed, living, uncontested headquarters supplies its original faction
through adjacent friendly-controlled, uncontested, traversable land. Roads are
optional. Mountains and other traversable land carry basic supply; water does not.
Ownership is never changed by isolation.

- Supplied city: 1 CR/s.
- Supplied completed, powered resource extractor: 2 CR/s.
- Continuous friendly roads from HQ: +10% to that city's or extractor's income.
- HQ building income remains 5 CR/s and receives no road bonus. A city under an
  HQ still earns its separate city income, including any applicable road bonus.
- An unfinished, captured, contested, sold or destroyed HQ is not a supply root.
  Without any active HQ, all city and extractor income stops.
- Construction, repair and training do not require supply in this slice. Existing
  power restrictions and contest rules still apply.

HQs, cities and extractor sites can act as road endpoints without having a road
underneath: one adjacent connected road segment is enough. Intermediate cells
must actually have roads. An unroaded city cannot bridge two disconnected roads.
Road capture can interrupt supply without deleting the road. An ordinary land
path can maintain basic income even when the road bonus is interrupted.

## Central calculation and cache

`EconomyState.income_rate()` remains the sole source of passive income credited
by `advance()`. `income_status(hex, tiles, territory)` returns `supplied`,
`road_bonus` and the current `income` for display; queries never credit funds.

`SupplyNetwork.refresh()` compares owned cells' ownership-derived membership,
contest, walkability, roads and terrain plus HQ completion/health eligibility and
economic endpoints. Changed inputs rebuild two bounded hex-neighbor graphs:
friendly land reachability and friendly road reachability. Identical inputs reuse
the graph. No whole-map flood fill runs every frame. Input validation still scans
owned cells and faction structures, so direct dictionary mutations are detected
without fragile revision-counter synchronization.

The cache is per economy, uses exact values rather than hash-only invalidation,
and is derived, not serialized. Existing saves contain everything needed to
reconstruct it. Loaded balances and ledgers are unchanged; subsequent income uses
the new supply rules. Existing infrastructure retains its original faction on
capture, consistent with prior slices.

## Interface

Selecting a city or extractor shows SUPPLIED or ISOLATED, current CR/s and the
road bonus in the sidebar. Faction income is displayed with two decimal places.
Isolated owned cities and extractors have a warning ring and crossed marker on
the map, beneath units/buildings. The marker represents actual isolation rather
than decorating every location. Existing sidebar styling is retained; no assets
or logistics controls were introduced.

## AI and recovery

The AI prioritizes an affordable replacement HQ before recruitment construction
when its HQ is missing. Zero funds, no HQ and no saleable assets cannot recover
by receiving unsupplied city income or a bailout. This is a deliberate consequence
of the requested economic rules. No delivery vehicles, fuel, shipments or manual
transport orders exist.
