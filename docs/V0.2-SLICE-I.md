# v0.2 Slice I: Production & Construction Interface

The economy sidebar lists every controlled structure, including foundations. Each row identifies its kind and axial coordinates, construction percentage or active production job and queue count, repairs, contest status, and applicable low-power penalty. An empty roster explicitly reports no controlled structures. The power summary uses the current faction grid, not cached estimates.

## Managing facilities

- Select a structure in the roster or click its map hex to pin its controls. Multiple factories remain separate entries, with independent queues and rally points.
- Inspecting empty terrain, selecting squads, or placing another building does not replace the pinned facility. Its world badge and rally line stay highlighted.
- Training buttons and I/T shortcuts target the pinned facility. The selected job has a progress bar; the detail text lists all queued jobs and its rally coordinates.
- Use **Set rally point on map**, then click or tap reachable land. The facility remains pinned. An invalid destination preserves its old rally and allows another attempt; Escape cancels destination selection. Right-click rally orders also target the pinned facility when no squad is selected.
- Foundations expose progress, health and partial-refund cancellation. Completed structures expose health, power usage/supply, paid repair/stop-repair and salvage controls under the existing rules.
- Selling, destruction, loss of ownership, or replacing a building instance clears its selection and stale actions. A replacement on the same hex must be selected explicitly. Load also invalidates old instance references; UI selection is not saved.

## Construction catalog

Expand **Available structures** to see all five kinds, purchase prices, construction durations, power supply/demand and placement requirements. Unaffordable choices and an already-present HQ are disabled with explanatory text. Choosing an available type enters the existing ghost placement workflow; actual placement still rechecks terrain, ownership, contest, occupancy and funds.

The structure roster has a bounded scrolling area; the overall sidebar scrolls separately so jobs, long queues, catalog text and action buttons remain reachable. Rows are retained between updates to preserve focus and scroll position rather than recreated every frame.

## Interface rationale

This is a functional extension of the existing strategy HUD, not a visual redesign. Its existing theme is retained. The roster groups sites by identity and coordinates because the task is choosing an exact facility, not a generic unit type. The collapsed catalog leaves room for live management. Text states accompany selection and power status so color is not the only signal. No new visual assets were created.

Visual direction and the requested afterward design audit remain pending; treat the visual presentation as a draft without direction (ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1). Headless tests do not establish visual or accessibility acceptance.

## Verification

Headless regression coverage exercises roster selection signals, independently queued factories and rally destinations, terrain inspection, construction while pinned, progress, catalog requirements, low-power status, repair controls, replacement identity, ownership loss and empty roster state. Existing cancellation, sale and save/load tests continue to exercise their HUD actions.

Manual acceptance remains pending: scroll a long roster/catalog on desktop and narrow viewports; operate the controls with keyboard and touch; verify focus, contrast, map input isolation, and that the pinned factory highlight and rally line stay readable. No interactive click-through or full design-gate pass is claimed.
