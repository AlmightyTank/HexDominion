# v0.5 Slice C: Neutral Oil Derricks

Slice C adds capturable neutral Oil Derricks as the first passive recurring income source. It preserves the physical supply loop and does not restore automatic income from cities, headquarters, territory or legacy extractors.

## Gameplay contract

- A new match places exactly 20 deterministic derricks on valid, reachable land.
- Placement excludes cities, resource and supply sites, named sites, starting units and starting buildings. Each faction receives a reachable early opportunity before farthest-point spacing distributes the remaining sites.
- Only stationary infantry advances capture. Capture takes five uninterrupted seconds.
- Opposing stationary ground forces defend or contest the site. Vehicles can defend but cannot capture.
- Completed capture changes the derrick owner and hex owner together.
- An operational owned derrick pays 3 credits per second. Full-continent bonuses are additive, while roads never add an oil payout bonus.
- Neutral, contested, actively capturing and destroyed derricks pay zero.
- Derricks have 300 HP. Destruction retains ownership, and the owner can restore the site at 20 HP per second for 1 credit per HP.

## Authoritative simulation

`OilDerrickNetwork` owns site identity, placement, capture, health, repairs and payout timing. Oil income enters `CreditsAccount` only through `EconomyState.credit_approved_income()` with the `oil_derrick` source.

Payout time and pending income are saved. One-second boundaries remain deterministic across frame sizes and save/load, so loading cannot repeat a prior payment. Oil remains separate from ordinary buildings and does not participate in building sale or production behavior.

## Player and AI behavior

- World rendering distinguishes neutral, player, rival, damaged, destroyed and selected states, with strategic icon art, health bars and capture progress.
- Inspection reports owner, HP, operational status, repairs and actual contribution per minute.
- The world summary reports operational site count and income per minute.
- Player-owned damaged or destroyed sites expose the existing paid repair control.
- Rival AI selects reachable non-owned sites for idle infantry, then uses ordinary pathfinding and movement. It cannot capture remotely.

## Save compatibility

Oil state was introduced in save schema v4. The current schema chain also contains later fields, so older schema-v3 saves receive deterministic neutral defaults while preserving balances, units and buildings. Validation rejects malformed IDs, invalid placement, duplicate sites, impossible capture state and invalid payout timers.

## Verification

Focused Oil Derrick tests cover placement, fair access, capture, recapture, contests, exact payouts, continent bonuses, no road bonus, destruction, repairs, save/load, AI routing and all artwork states. The smoke suite also checks legacy migration and malformed save rejection.
