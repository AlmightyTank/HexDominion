# v0.4 Slice B: vehicles and hex armies

## Vehicle roles

All four roles are trained at completed, controlled factories through the existing queue and power rules. Costs and statistics live in `UnitCatalog`; movement, combat and production share those definitions.

| Role | Credits / training | HP | Seconds per plain hex | Damage / cooldown | Range | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main battle tank | 150 / 8s | 250 | 0.65 | 22.5 / 0.5s | 1 | Armored direct fire, separate chassis and turret headings |
| Scout vehicle | 80 / 4s | 100 | 0.30 | 4 / 0.2s | 1 | Fast reconnaissance and capture, weak against armor |
| Armored personnel carrier | 120 / 6s | 200 | 0.50 | 4 / 0.4s | 1 | Four infantry passengers, light supporting weapon |
| Mobile anti-air | 140 / 7s | 180 | 0.60 | 10 / 0.8s | 3 | Prioritizes airborne targets; weak against ground armor |

Vehicles use armor terrain costs and existing roads, land restrictions, capture and hold rules. Infantry-only bunkers remain infantry-only. Tank damage multipliers are 0.5 against infantry and 1.5 against armor. Scout multipliers are 1 / 0.3, APC 0.8 / 0.25, and anti-air 0.4 / 0.15. Anti-air's airborne multiplier is 3. Aircraft production is not part of this slice; synthetic airborne targets verify that prerequisite targeting behavior.

## Stack-first presentation

The approved direction is one deployed stack per faction per hex at every zoom. Its icon represents the most numerous type, its number counts all deployed members, and catalog order resolves ties deterministically. Friendly and hostile stacks remain separate. Markers stay anchored to their logical hex; movement still advances individual simulation objects normally.

Click a friendly marker to select the whole stack. The existing sidebar shows its complete type counts, not just the selected subset. Click a type row to select only that type, Select all to restore the whole hex army, or Expand to reveal individually pickable units. Collapse restores the shared marker. Shift-click/Add selection adds or removes a whole stack. Box selection tests the same displayed anchors. Buildings remain independently managed through the existing pinned facility selection.

APC passengers are listed separately as aboard, never included in deployed counts. Full primary-unit statistics remain available in the army heading tooltip. The bounded, scrollable composition panel remains visible independently of the production tab. Stable controls preserve keyboard focus while counts change.

Existing faction/state SVG assets are reused for scouts, APCs, anti-air and strategic icons. Expanded tanks retain a tracked hull with independently aimed turret, movement tracks, recoil, damage feedback and selection/health indicators. No new image assets were generated. Shared-marker health uses the lowest member health fraction, so damaged units do not disappear behind a healthy representative.

## Transport

- Select infantry, choose Load APC (L), then click the friendly stack containing a stationary APC on the same hex. At most four infantry can board. If several APCs share a stack, the first available stationary carrier is used.
- Select an APC through its composition row. Choose Unload APC (U), then click its hex or adjacent land. All passengers deploy holding position; ocean and distant destinations are rejected without changing cargo.
- Passengers follow their carrier but cannot move independently, fire, be targeted, capture, garrison, render or be selected while aboard.
- Destroying an APC kills all its passengers in the same cleanup tick. There is no emergency ejection or refund.
- Invalid loading/unloading leaves the mode active for retry; Escape cancels it. There is no automatic rendezvous, automatic loading or enemy boarding.

Save snapshots preserve carrier roster indices, vehicle identity, cooldown progress and turret heading. Validation rejects overcapacity, foreign/non-APC/self carriers, malformed headings, moving passengers and invalid indices before mutating the match. Older snapshots default to no passengers and use chassis heading for the turret. Presentation expansion and stack selection are transient, not saved.

## Verification and scope

Run the existing smoke, HUD and artwork suites plus:

```text
godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/vehicle_tests.gd
godot --path . --rendering-method gl_compatibility --script res://tests/vehicle_visual_test.gd -- --output=<directory>
```

Coverage includes both factions' production/costs, terrain/roads, counters and targeting, transport capacity and invalid orders, inactive passengers, destruction cleanup, save validation/legacy defaults, dominant-role ties, exact composition counts, type selection, whole-stack selection, expansion and rendered-anchor picking at three zoom levels. Rendered fixtures capture stacks, expanded vehicles, firing, damage, movement and unloading. The GitHub workflow runs both new suites and uploads their PNGs with the existing visual artifacts.

Local verification: 1,190 smoke checks, 134 vehicle checks and 724 artwork checks passed with zero failures. The HUD suite passed. The real OpenGL vehicle fixture captured all views with zero failures; firing feedback is compared against the same expanded idle presentation.

The interface retains the established RTS HUD direction (ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1). One marker reduces map clutter; type rows expose exact composition without another popup. Headless signal tests and renderer captures are evidence, not physical mouse/touch or accessibility acceptance. Full afterward UI audit and balance playtesting remain pending. The rival planner retains its existing infantry/tank purchasing policy; it does not yet coordinate APC transport or purchase every new vehicle role.
