# v0.2 Slice F: Cancellation and refunds

Inspect a controlled unfinished construction site, then choose **Cancel construction (+N CR)** in the sidebar. The amount shown is 50% of its placement cost: HQ 150 CR, barracks 50 CR, factory 100 CR, extractor 50 CR. Progress and damage do not change the refund. This is distinct from cancelling an unplaced ghost, which never spent credits.

Cancellation removes the site immediately and records the refund in the faction's credits account. Its hex becomes available for construction, including release of the one-HQ restriction if the cancelled site was an HQ. There are no separate occupancy reservations to leave behind.

## Restrictions and safeguards

- Only unfinished sites belonging to the faction on currently controlled land qualify. Contested sites may be cancelled while control is retained.
- Completed buildings, destroyed sites and structures on lost territory cannot be cancelled for a construction refund.
- The sidebar hides the action for completed or absent sites. Main-scene commands are blocked while paused or after the match ends.
- The economy rechecks completion, ownership and the identity of the exact site before issuing credits. Repeated requests do nothing; a stale request cannot cancel a new site on the same hex, or one reconstructed by loading a save.
- The refund must succeed before the site is removed. At the credits ceiling, a rejected refund leaves the site intact.
- Saves already serialize both buildings and the account ledger. Saving after cancellation therefore retains the refund and the absence of the site without a separate refund flag.

## Verification

Headless tests cover both factions and all building types, damaged partial sites, exact refunds, repeated requests, immediate rebuilding, released HQ limits, stale requests against replacements, lost ownership, completion, destruction and refund overflow. Integration coverage invokes the sidebar button on a loaded partial site, verifies immediate HUD cleanup, then saves/restores the cancelled state and rejects another refund.

Interactive visual acceptance remains pending: inspect an unfinished site, confirm the displayed amount, cancel it, and check that its foundation disappears and credits increase once. Completed structures must not show the cancellation button.
