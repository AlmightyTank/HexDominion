# v0.2 Slice D: Building placement

Choose a building in the construction sidebar, or use H (HQ), B (barracks), F (factory), E (extractor). Selection does not spend credits. The ghost snaps to the hovered hex: teal means valid, red means blocked. The sidebar also states VALID or BLOCKED, so color is not the only feedback.

Left-click confirms placement. Q rotates the silhouette clockwise through six 60-degree orientations. Every current building occupies one hex; rotation is visual and does not change production, combat or footprint. Right-click or Escape cancels without spending. Escape outside placement still opens the pause menu. Touch taps place; pans and pinches do not confirm construction.

## Placement rules

- The hex must exist, be land, belong to the player and not be contested.
- Neither faction may already have a structure there, including inactive captured structures.
- Extractors require resource terrain. Only one HQ is permitted per faction.
- Confirmation rechecks funds and all placement rules before a single debit. Invalid placement keeps the ghost active and does not spend credits.
- Successful placement leaves placement mode. Building orientation is saved; older saves without an orientation default to zero.
- The sidebar remains available without an inspected tile. Inspecting an owned production building still exposes its training actions.
