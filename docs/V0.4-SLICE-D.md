# v0.4 Slice D: Aircraft and airfields

## Integration and controls

Airfields use the existing paid placement, foundations, repair, cancellation, salvage, ownership, destruction and production queues. Select **Airfield** under Structures. A runway needs controlled, uncontested, empty plains/desert/city land (including a resource hex with an eligible underlying biome). Cost: 250 CR; construction: 12 seconds; health: 350; power use: 20. Building a runway does not require spare power, allowing recovery planning, but production and fighter rearming stop under low power or local contest. Aircraft already flying are unaffected by outages.

Produce aircraft under **Vehicles / Aircraft**. A selected airfield, otherwise the primary/newest compatible airfield, supplies the independent queue. Helicopters obey its rally, including water; fighters wait at their base. Select an aircraft on the map, right-click to move, or use Attack / right-click an enemy to attack. The selection inspector shows the assigned base, mission state and remaining rearm time. Selecting an air marker does not also select the ground army underneath. Shift selection, box selection, health, faction colors and existing order controls remain available.

All tuning lives in `UnitCatalog`. Helicopter counters: infantry x1, armor x1.5. Fighter counters: infantry x0.8, armor/air x1.5, structures x1.5. Ground cover still protects ground targets; aircraft receive no ground-terrain cover. Mobile AA prioritizes aircraft and deals x3 damage to them. Other existing ground weapons cannot target aircraft. Fighters can target ground, structures or hostile aircraft; helicopters target ground and structures. Both are non-capturing units, not transports or garrison occupants.

## Missions and failure handling

- Aircraft use map-bounded flight paths over land and water at a flat edge cost, without roads, mountains, rivers or terrain movement penalties. No fuel, landing pathfinding, collision blocking or off-map flight is introduced.
- Helicopters hover and fight using existing weapon ranges, continuous orders and cooldown volleys. They do not require a surviving airfield to continue flying.
- Fighters need a ready mission and assigned base to accept an attack. They approach weapon range, fire exactly one targeted strike, return to their base, then rearm there for a configurable **8 seconds**. Bulk simulation deltas cannot grant multiple strikes. Idle, returning and rearming fighters do not auto-fire.
- A destroyed/lost target aborts the strike and returns the fighter. A sold, destroyed, contested or captured airfield invalidates the assignment. Aircraft adopt the nearest eligible friendly completed airfield, using stable building order for ties. A fighter with no base holds airborne in `orphaned` state and cannot launch another strike. It resumes returning when a replacement base becomes available. No aircraft is silently deleted or given free repairs.
- Movement/Hold can cancel an active strike, but cannot reset its rearm requirement. Replacing an invalid base interrupts rearming and requires a full rearm at the new base. Airfields do not transfer faction infrastructure on capture, consistent with existing structures.

## Presentation

The existing original helicopter and swept-wing fighter SVGs, faction colors and damage states are reused, not overwritten. A new runway/hangar/tower SVG identifies airfields. Aircraft stacks are separated by faction and aircraft type, independently of ground stacks. Ground draws first, aircraft afterward. Far zoom uses type markers with faction colors and real group counts. Medium/close zoom draws independent aircraft with altitude offsets, shadows, animated main/tail helicopter rotors, smooth heading changes during flight, muzzle flashes and missile/impact feedback. Ground vehicles retain their existing silhouettes and stack-first behavior.

This extends the existing RTS HUD direction (`docs/RTS-HUD.md`), not a redesign: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 for HUD chrome. Gameplay motion communicates flight, firing and altitude; it freezes with the existing pause controls. Existing SVG silhouettes provide recognizable roles without a new unrelated icon library. Aircraft offset and draw order distinguish air from ground; real counts preserve roster clarity. Damage is resolved on the volley, not on the presentation-only missile impact. No splash, dogfight physics, manual fuel or automatic rival air-purchase strategy is included.

## Persistence and verification

Version-one snapshots add optional base coordinate, mission state and remaining rearm time. Domains derive from catalog kinds. Validation permits water paths only for aircraft, checks bounded adjacent steps, finite cooldowns/base types/mission states and disallows embarked/garrisoned aircraft. Missing bases can safely remain pending until the next simulation update. Legacy ground saves default to neutral aircraft fields. Rotors, shadows and missile effects are derived/transient; loading cannot duplicate damage or production.
