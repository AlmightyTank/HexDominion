# v0.3 Slice A: Advanced Terrain Rules

## Movement and pathfinding

`TerrainRules` defines movement and combat modifiers. `HexPathfinding.find_path`
uses A* to minimize travel cost for the specified unit kind, with a binary heap,
a conservative minimum-cost heuristic and deterministic insertion-order ties.
Player orders, production rallies and AI movement use the same costs.

Entering a hex multiplies the unit's base seconds per hex by:

| Terrain | Rifle infantry | Tank |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Plains | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Forest | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Mountain | 1.25 | 1.25 |
| Desert | 1.0 | 1 / 1.1 |
| City | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Snow | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Tundra | 1.0 | 1.0 |

Mountain speed is 80% of normal; desert vehicle speed is 110% of normal.
Connected roads divide total edge time (including crossings) by 1.3.

Resource sites retain their underlying biome's rules. Unknown or absent terrain
metadata defaults to plains, preserving simple land fixtures. Cities explicitly
use city rules rather than the natural ground color under their map marker.

Additional edge costs, added before multiplying by base movement time:

| Crossing | Rifle infantry | Tank |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| River | +0.6 | +1.0 |
| Narrow land crossing | +0.4 | +0.75 |

A crossing touching a narrow hex pays the narrow penalty once, not once per
bank. River and narrow penalties can stack. A narrow hex has one or two adjacent
land exits; this identifies coastline-defined bottlenecks, not bridges or
mountain-pass topology. Both penalties work in either direction.

**Mountains restrict armor through travel time, not a hard ban.** Every existing
land route remains legal for both unit types, including sole mountain passes,
river crossings and choke points. Ocean, off-map and disconnected destinations
remain invalid. Rejected orders retain the previous route.

`UnitState.advance(delta, tiles)` consumes real seconds separately for each edge,
including leftover time across terrain changes. Mid-edge redirects finish the
current edge without teleporting or resetting fractional progress. Large and
partitioned ticks produce the same movement result. Logical occupancy remains
the last reached hex until arrival.

## Combat

Stationary defenders multiply incoming damage by:

| Position | Rifle infantry | Tank |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Forest | 0.85 | 1.00 |
| Mountain | 0.75 | 0.75 |
| City | 0.80 | 0.80 |
| Other terrain | 1.00 | 1.00 |

Units with an active move route receive no stationary cover. No terrain grants
an attack bonus. Snow and tundra are neutral for movement and combat. For example,
a tank firing on stationary city infantry deals `45 * 0.8 = 36` damage per second.

Terrain-aware unit damage still resolves simultaneously with stable target
selection. Moving units do not fire. Terrain attack modifiers also apply when
firing on buildings; buildings keep their existing structural health rules and
do not receive unit cover bonuses. Rivers change movement, not weapon range or
capture duration.

## River data and presentation

The bundled public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m river centerlines are projected
into the map's existing normalized coordinates. Intersections with adjacent
land-center segments mark symmetric river crossings. The source and license
are recorded in [the river source notes](../data/world/RIVERS.md).

River edges use blue strokes in the existing cached terrain layer so players can
identify the crossings that slow troops. Existing terrain colors and symbols
remain unchanged. No live terrain-cache invalidation or external service is
required. These are coarse world-scale crossings, not detailed waterways,
bridges or a naval navigation network.

## Saves and integration

Terrain metadata is deterministic map data, not mutable match state. Existing
save schemas remain compatible: routes, logical hexes and edge progress are
already saved. Loading reconstructs identical crossings and costs; existing
saved routes remain legal and new orders optimize using the terrain rules.
Previously saved fractional edge progress now uses the new edge duration.
Construction, power, credits and capture rules are otherwise unchanged.

```powershell
godot --headless --path . --editor --quit
godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/run_smoke_tests.gd
godot --headless --path . --quit-after 10
```

The dedicated terrain suite covers:

- Exact infantry/armor movement costs and mountain penalties without eliminating
  sole routes; shortest travel-time routing rather than hex counts.
- River and narrow costs, symmetric crossings, stacked penalties, ocean and
  off-map rejection, and traversal of every land terrain type.
- A* optimality against independent exhaustive relaxation over mixed-terrain
  maps, plus deterministic repeated routing for both unit kinds.
- Per-edge timing, fractional movement, mixed-edge leftover time, large ticks,
  tick partitioning, redirects and invalid-order retention.
- Infantry-specific cover, mountain/city defenders, moving defenders, neutral
  snow/tundra combat, desert armor, combined modifiers and building attacks.
- Real river adjacency, symmetry, walkability and deterministic map rebuilding.

The full runner also retains mid-move save/load comparisons and full-match AI,
economy and victory regressions using the live terrain-aware simulation.
