# v0.4 Slice E: Naval Warfare and Amphibious Invasions

## Construction and deployment

A shipyard costs 220 CR, takes 10 seconds and has 400 HP. Place it on owned, uncontested, empty coastal land adjoining navigable ocean. It uses the same foundation, cancellation, repair, sale and destruction systems as other structures. It requires no electricity. Completed shipyards produce naval units through the existing vehicle production list and can be made primary production sites.

Deployment uses the first navigable adjacent ocean hex in stable hex-direction order. A contested/lost yard or blocked deployment pauses its paid queue without banking production time. Ships never spawn on land. Set a rally on reachable water; land and disconnected-water destinations are rejected. Without a water rally, ships wait at their deployment hex. Water is a map tile with `is_land=false`, ocean terrain, and no `navigable=false` restriction. No artificial land bridges or road changes are introduced.

Tuning lives in `UnitCatalog`. Ships use water-only A*, independent of roads and land terrain costs. They can be attacked by applicable ground/air weapons in range; they cannot target aircraft. Automatic stationary combat and simultaneous cooldown volleys retain their previous rules. Explicit destroyer building bombardment keeps the ordered structure target instead of diverting to defenders. Naval units never capture territory.

## Amphibious controls and shared cargo

1. Bring stationary infantry or permitted vehicles to coastal land; park a friendly landing craft in adjacent ocean.
2. Select the ground army, press **L / Load transport**, then click the landing craft.
3. Move the selected craft across reachable ocean.
4. Press **U / Unload transport**, then choose adjacent clear coastal land. All cargo deploys there and holds position. Ground units then capture land normally.

Infantry cost one slot. Tanks, scouts, empty APCs, mobile AA and artillery cost two. Aircraft, ships, nested occupied transports, enemy cargo, moving units and over-capacity orders are rejected. An APC still carries four same-hex infantry using the same `TransportRules`; there is no second cargo implementation. No automatic coastal rendezvous is performed.

Landings reject water, off-map/distant land, `landable=false` tiles and land occupied by living deployed enemy ground units. Friendly stacks and buildings remain passable, as elsewhere. Landing is all-or-nothing. Clear defended beaches through bombardment before unloading. Loaded units cannot move, shoot, capture, be selected or count as deployed stacks. Destruction loses all remaining cargo with no salvage or automatic ejection, matching APC policy.

## Presentation and saves

Reuses the repository's original, distinct faction/state naval SVGs: slim patrol hull/light gun, long destroyer with multiple guns, broad landing craft with cargo deck/ramp. No recolored generic replacement or external assets. Far zoom shows separate role/faction markers and real deployed counts. Medium and close zoom draw individual directional ships above ocean terrain, with wakes, weapon flashes, selection/health and landing-craft slot counts. Ground stack behavior is unchanged; existing army detail and load/unload controls handle naval selection.

The existing version-1 save format retains cargo roster indices, ship kind/position/path/progress/heading/health and shipyard queues/rallies. Naval and embarked-ground positions may be ocean; unembarked ground units still cannot be saved there. Validation rejects land-based ships, invalid paths, foreign/self/nested carriers, excess weighted capacity and invalid shipyard/rally locations before changing live state. Old saves without naval units keep their previous behavior. Visual wake/flash state is transient.

## Verification and limits

`tests/naval_tests.gd` covers both factions, paid coastal construction, production/deployment, captured/blocked yards, water routing, weighted cargo, duplicate/moving/invalid loads, blocked landings, cross-continent ground capture, destruction, naval/coastal combat and snapshot validation/round trips. `tests/naval_visual_test.gd` captures far/medium/close silhouettes, passenger counts, wakes and firing using the real renderer. Both run in GitHub validation; images join its visual-review artifact.

Local verification: 116 naval checks, 1280 smoke/simulation checks, 134 vehicle checks, 91 aircraft checks, 73 artillery checks and 784 artwork checks passed; HUD regressions and rendered naval fixtures passed. These are automated simulations/handlers, not physical input acceptance. A clean staged export is also validated before committing.

Existing RTS direction is retained (ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1). Wakes communicate movement, gun flashes identify actual volleys, and faction markings/health/selection communicate ownership and state. No unrelated HUD redesign is included. Physical touchscreen testing, full accessibility/contrast review, competitive balance and the requested afterward antislop audit remain pending. The rival planner does not yet purchase ships or organize invasions. There is no naval minefield, fuel, collision avoidance, sea-territory capture, splash or line-of-sight simulation; shared water stacks are legal.
