# v0.2 Slice B — Territory Income

- Each owned, uncontested city earns **1 credit/second** for its owner, identically for player and AI.
- Ordinary hexes and resource hexes generate **no passive territory income**. Ownership still matters strategically and for construction. Resource exploitation is not implemented yet.
- Existing HQ income remains an explicit **5 credits/second building bonus**, not a property of ordinary land. A city with an HQ earns 6 credits/second total.
- Stationary, living hostile occupants interrupt city income immediately on the next simulation tick, including mixed-faction contests with no capture timer yet. Moving units follow the existing capture rule and do not contest until they stop. Income is also interrupted while hostile capture progress exists.
- Capture completion immediately enables the new owner's city income. Removing hostile occupants or abandoning a capture restores the existing owner's income; a friendly garrison is not required. HQ bonuses are likewise suspended while contested.
- Territory updates before both faction economies each physics tick, and HUD rates are derived directly from current ownership/contest state. No cached income rate or delayed payout timer is used.
- Saves preserve contest state as an optional version-1 field; older saves remain compatible and rebuild contests from occupants on the next tick. Existing capture progress already suppresses income immediately after loading.

Headless tests cover neutral/owned cities, fractional income, resource/plain non-income, capture/loss transfer, contested occupation before capture starts, abandoned captures, dead occupants, HQ bonuses, and parity between player and AI. Existing skirmish, save/load and ledger regression tests remain included. Interactive visual checking remains pending.
