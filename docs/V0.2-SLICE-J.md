# v0.2 Slice J: AI, Balancing & Integration

## Opponent economy

`RivalEconomyAI` derives its priorities from live territory, structures, queues,
credits and units. It uses the same `EconomyState` purchase, sale, repair, income,
power and construction rules as the player. There are no free recovery credits,
instant structures, discounted units or ownership bypasses. Player structures
also block AI placement after their hex changes hands.

After the existing eight-second opening grace period, decisions run approximately
once per second. The planner:

1. Restores missing recruitment infrastructure. If there is no army or queued
   replacement and recruitment cannot be funded or placed, it can sell controlled,
   completed optional infrastructure at the normal 50% salvage rate.
2. Replaces a missing HQ when affordable, respecting the one-HQ rule. A captured
   surviving HQ must be recaptured, not duplicated.
3. Funds power before additional consumers or extraction. Paid foundations count
   toward planned supply/demand, avoiding duplicate plant orders while one builds.
4. Builds extractors on controlled, uncontested, unoccupied deposits.
5. Adds a factory after establishing an army, then recruits a mix with up to two
   tanks. Normal recruitment stops at eight living plus controlled queued units.
6. Enables paid repairs on damaged completed infrastructure when credits exceed
   200, and pauses them below that budget on subsequent decisions. Repairs retain
   normal health limits, ownership rules and per-HP charges.

Optional recruitment reserves funds for missing HQs, power, extraction and the
first factory. The last replacement scout is exempt from these savings reserves.
This prevents low city income from being spent on an endless stream of rifles
before recovery investments become affordable. Prices and player income rates
remain unchanged: HQ 5 CR/s, city 1 CR/s, powered extractor 2 CR/s.

## Territory and fighting

The first living rival unit is the economic scout. It prioritizes reachable lost
core infrastructure, maintains spare nearby construction land, then seeks
reachable unowned cities and deposits. A breadth-first search excludes isolated
objectives rather than repeatedly issuing impossible orders. Other units retain
the HQ advance. All units react to nearby player units, hold to fire in range,
and finish captures before moving on. They also hold to destroy hostile nearby
structures, freeing captured resource deposits for their own extractors.

Recruits deploy from their production building and receive orders at the next AI
decision. Capture, combat, destruction, timed construction and low-power effects
continue through the existing shared simulation.

## Save/load and regression coverage

There is no unsaved planner state or new save schema. Existing saved accounts,
queues, construction, repairs, ownership, roster and AI countdown are sufficient
to resume decisions. Infrastructure and units are never cached across a load.

`tests/rival_economy_tests.gd` is called by the existing smoke runner. It exercises:

- A four-minute economy simulation that builds a factory, plant and extractor,
  captures a deposit, trains armor, earns income and respects the recruitment cap.
- Plant destruction and rebuilding; paid repair completion without overhealing.
- Zero-cash recovery from surviving city income, rebuilding recruitment and HQ
  income instead of starving recovery purchases with continuous recruitment.
- Emergency salvage at normal prices, unreachable objectives, core recapture,
  captured enemy extractors, invalid deltas and overlapping foreign structures.
- Repeated build/cancel/stale-sale requests for both factions, paid queued-unit
  sales, ledger integrity and no refunds or orphan recruits after cleanup.

The main runner also starts two real map scenes, builds player production, saves
mid-expansion with damaged AI infrastructure and active production, restores the
second scene, and runs both for six simulated minutes. Periodic snapshots must
remain valid and identical; the match must reach the same latched result and stop
all simulation mutations after completion. Existing slot-file corruption,
backup recovery and save validation regressions remain part of the suite.

## Limits and manual acceptance

- This is a deterministic baseline, not difficulty tuning or a competitive AI.
  It has one economic scout, no fog of war and no complex multi-front planning.
- Full loss of income, affordable assets and all units cannot be reversed by
  either faction without breaking the shared economic rules. No bailout is
  minted for that terminal resource-exhaustion state; recovery tests retain a
  real city income source or enough salvage to pay for recruitment.
- The existing HQ-territory win/loss rule is unchanged. Destroying or selling an
  HQ alone does not move the objective or immediately end the match.
- Interactive playtesting is still required for perceived difficulty, expansion
  pacing and whether economic raids feel worthwhile. Headless regression results
  are not a claim of visual acceptance or final gameplay balance.
- No UI redesign or assets are introduced by this slice. The previously requested
  afterward UI audit remains separate and pending.
