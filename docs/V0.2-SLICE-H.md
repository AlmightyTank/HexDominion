# v0.2 Slice H: Repairing and selling buildings

Inspect a controlled completed building to access maintenance actions in the sidebar. Damaged buildings show **Repair (1 CR / HP)**, active repairs show **Stop repairs**, and completed structures show **Sell building (+N CR)**. Foundations continue to use construction cancellation instead.

## Repairs

- Repairs restore at most 20 HP per second and cost 1 credit per HP actually restored. Starting and stopping a job have no fee.
- Health never exceeds the building type's maximum. The final partial repair charges only the missing HP, then stops automatically.
- Insufficient credits limit restoration to the affordable amount. The job pauses at zero funds and resumes when funds return; it cannot overdraw the account.
- Repairing pauses on lost or contested land. The original faction's job resumes after control returns. Stop-repair requires current control.
- Repairs work during low power and do not interrupt normal production. Building health bars and the sidebar show remaining health.
- Active repair flags and damaged health persist in snapshots. Legacy saves without repair flags default to no repair job. Invalid flag types and repair jobs on foundations are rejected.

## Selling and cleanup

Selling a completed controlled building refunds 50% of its original placement cost: HQ 150, barracks 50, factory 100, extractor 50 and power plant 75 credits. Damage does not change salvage. Foundations cannot be sold; their existing cancellation action remains separate.

The refund is recorded before removing the building. If the account rejects the refund at its ceiling, the structure remains. Requests identify the exact live building, so duplicate clicks and stale requests against replacements or reloaded state cannot issue extra salvage.

Selling removes the building, its repair job, rally data and paid production queue. Queued unit costs are not refunded, and removed queues cannot spawn recruits later. Its power contribution and hex occupancy disappear immediately. The same cleanup happens on destruction, but destruction awards no salvage. All completed building types can now be targeted by enemy units, retaining unit-combat priority.

HQs may be sold or destroyed, and a replacement may be built under the existing one-HQ rule. The match's original HQ hexes remain capture objectives; selling or destroying an HQ does not itself cause victory or relocate that objective. Saves now permit a faction to have zero HQ buildings while retaining its objective hex.

## Verification

Headless tests cover every building type for both factions: repair start/stop, fractional spending, full-health rejection, contested pause, max-health clamping, affordable partial healing, funding recovery, exact salvage, repeated sale rejection, stale replacement requests and destruction during repair. Tests also verify removal of paid production queues.

Integration coverage invokes stop-repair and sale through the sidebar on loaded state, saves/restores sold state, rejects repeat salvage and validates a match after selling its HQ. Existing continuation tests include an active repair job and credits ledger.

Interactive visual checks remain pending: start and stop repairs while watching health/credits, sell a queued factory, sell a power plant during production, and inspect sidebar layout at narrow resolutions.
