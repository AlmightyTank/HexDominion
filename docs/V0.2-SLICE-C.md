# v0.2 Slice C — Resource Extraction

Resource terrain now represents deposits. Deposits alone still earn nothing: capture one, then use its construction menu or **E** to build an **extractor (100 credits)**. Extractors have **200 HP** and earn **2 credits/second** while alive, on a resource tile owned by their original faction, and uncontested. Partial damage does not reduce output. No trucks, finite deposit depletion, power, repairs or upkeep yet.

Enemy stationary units automatically attack extractors in their normal attack range, using their normal DPS. Enemy units take targeting priority; an attacker never fires at a unit and extractor in the same tick. Moving/dead units do not attack. Extractor damage is aggregated before application. At zero HP the structure is removed without a refund, immediately stopping its income and freeing the tile for rebuilding at full cost. Other building types remain capture objectives rather than destructible structures in this slice.

Losing or contesting a deposit stops its extractor income immediately; recapturing it resumes surviving infrastructure. Ownership does not transfer an extractor to the captor. Player and AI economies support the same build, income and damage rules; the existing basic AI does not yet plan extractor construction or raids. Player-built extractors can be attacked automatically by passing enemy troops.

The construction menu validates both funds and deposit terrain. Extractors show a drill badge, HP bar, and extraction status in the tile panel. Health and infrastructure persist in named saves; invalid extractor health or non-resource placement is rejected on load. Destroyed structures remain absent after saving/loading.

Headless tests cover both factions' deposit/ownership checks, debit and duplicate construction, fractional income, contest/loss/recapture, damage, unit-target priority, moving attackers, destruction and rebuilding. Existing match save/load regressions also remain covered. Interactive visual/playtesting remains pending.
