# v0.5 Slice A: Economy Foundation and Starting Funds

## Scope

Slice A retires the old passive headquarters, city and extractor income model without replacing the existing wallet, production, construction, repair, sale, power, territory or military systems. It prepares the data and payout boundaries needed by physical supply deliveries in Slice B and captured oil derricks in Slice C.

The complete logistics economy is not playable in this slice. Until a delivery is completed or an operational oil derrick exists, recurring income is intentionally zero.

## Starting state

Every new match creates the same economic opportunity for both factions:

- exactly 2,000 credits in the existing faction `CreditsAccount`;
- one completed headquarters;
- one completed Supply Center;
- one unarmed Supply Truck; and
- one nearby finite supply deposit containing 5,000 supply units.

The two deposit sites use the same hex distance from their respective headquarters. Selection is deterministic: valid candidates are sorted by axial coordinates and the first candidate is used. Supply Centers, trucks and deposits are added only by `UnitLayer.configure()` during new-match setup. They do not spend starting credits. Save restoration reconstructs the saved roster and buildings directly and never runs this setup path, so it cannot duplicate starting assets.

The Supply Center and Supply Truck are valid saved game objects, but the truck does not collect or deliver supplies yet. The truck is unarmed and is not available in production queues in Slice A.

## Income rules

Headquarters, cities, resource terrain, extractors, roads, ordinary territory and completed buildings generate no credits by themselves. `EconomyState.income_rate()` remains as a compatibility query and always returns zero.

Only `EconomyState.credit_approved_income()` can record future recurring economy payouts. It accepts these source identifiers:

- `supply_delivery`, reserved for a completed physical delivery in Slice B; and
- `oil_derrick`, reserved for an operational captured derrick in Slice C.

Cancellation refunds and building-sale proceeds continue to use the existing account transaction system. They are one-time returns, not recurring economy income, and are excluded from the HUD's recent-income total.

### Bonus calculation

An approved payout is calculated once:

```text
payout = base_amount * (1 + road_bonus + continent_bonus)
road_bonus = 0.10 only for a completed delivery whose submitted route qualifies
continent_bonus = 0.10 * fully_controlled_continent_count
```

Bonuses are additive. A 100-credit delivery with a qualifying road route and two controlled continents pays `100 * (1 + 0.10 + 0.20) = 130` credits. An oil derrick payout never receives the road bonus. The resulting amount is credited in one account transaction and stored as one timed income event, preventing connectivity queries from applying or repeating a bonus.

`SupplyNetwork.qualifies_delivery_route()` requires every submitted route hex to be contiguous, friendly-owned, uncontested, walkable and roaded. Road connectivity alone never calls the payout API. The existing 30% road movement bonus is unchanged.

## HUD

The resource bar reports:

- available credits;
- approved income actually received during the latest 60 simulation seconds;
- living, deployed Supply Trucks;
- operational oil derrick count; and
- whether a working income source currently exists.

At the start of Slice A this reads 2,000 credits, zero recent income, one truck, zero derricks and no working source. Supply and continent panels describe future payout qualification without presenting connectivity or ownership as income.

## Save compatibility

Match saves now use schema version 2. Version 2 stores finite deposit state and the approved-income activity clock/events. New-match tests enforce exactly one free scaffold Supply Center and Supply Truck per faction. Save validation permits future legitimately produced trucks and centers; duplication is prevented by never running new-match setup during restoration.

Version 1 saves migrate in memory to version 2. Migration preserves the saved account balance, transaction history, units and buildings, enables the zero-passive-income rules, and initializes the new activity fields to validated empty defaults. It does not inject missing Supply Centers, Supply Trucks or deposits into an old match. This avoids duplicate assets and avoids resetting earned or spent credits. A migrated legacy match can therefore continue with its old asset layout but will have no recurring income before Slice B/C sources are implemented.

Malformed saves, saves for another map and saves from a newer schema are rejected by the existing validated load path. The primary and backup files remain unchanged when validation fails.

## Preserved systems

- one `CreditsAccount` per faction with atomic spending and nonnegative balances;
- construction and unit-production queues;
- cancellation refunds, repair spending and building-sale salvage;
- transaction history;
- power supply/demand and low-power production behavior;
- territory ownership, capture and continent control;
- `SupplyNetwork` connectivity;
- road movement bonuses; and
- existing combat, orders, AI and victory behavior.

The rival economy AI no longer spends toward passive extractors and receives no bailout. It spends the same finite 2,000-credit starting balance under the same account rules as the player.

## Verification targets

Automated coverage checks exact 2,000-credit starts, equivalent deterministic scaffolding, zero passive income, nonnegative atomic spending, refunds and sale proceeds, route qualification, additive one-time bonuses, save migration, no duplicated starting assets and the existing full-match simulation. Godot 4.7.2 headless import and repository test commands are the release gate for this slice.
